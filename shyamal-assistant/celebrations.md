# Celebrations (`celebrations.md`)

Loaded for birthday, anniversary, milestone, promotion, new role, or personal congratulatory wishes.

> [!CAUTION]
> **STRICT NO-SEND RULE**
> Never send any birthday, anniversary, or celebratory message directly. Always show the recipient (`To`), `Channel`, and the full text in chat first so Sudeep can copy-paste it (for WhatsApp/LinkedIn/text) or ask to save it as a Gmail Draft.

## Narrow Fetch (One Parallel Pass)
Check `relationships/` for the person's file, search Gmail (`from:<person> OR to:<person>`, last 90 days) and Calendar (`gcalendar`) for any recent shared context (trips, family mentions, hobbies, recent milestones, role changes).

## Voice Rules (LOCKED: No Cheese, No Dull Stock Lines)
Two failure modes, both unacceptable:
1. **Cheesy:** Eulogy-style character praise, cataloguing struggles, `"that's who you are"`, `"you've earned it"`, `"let yourself be celebrated"`, `"you make hard problems look calm"`.
2. **Dull:** A bare `"Happy birthday! Hope you have a wonderful day!"` with nothing specific or warm around it.

Aim for **fun, warm, and specific**: a light playful line, one real detail about the person (food, trip, hobby, family, or recent milestone), `!!` where natural for Sudeep's warmth, and zero corporate stiffness.

### Personal vs. Professional Occasions
- **Birthdays & personal anniversaries stay 100% personal.** Day, food, cake, family, hobbies, trips, time off, the year ahead. Test: *"Would I say this if we never worked together?"* If no, cut it.
- **Career milestones, new roles, promotions, or project launches:** Reference one real, specific win or trait, plus a warm forward-looking wish.

## Output Format
Always show a **Short Group/Public Version** (1–3 lively lines for a group chat or LinkedIn comment) AND a **Personal DM Version** (3–5 warmer sentences for WhatsApp/DM/Email):

```markdown
**Outbound Preview (Draft Only: Will Not Send)**
- **To:** <Person Name>
- **Occasion:** <Birthday / Anniversary / Milestone>
- **Grounding used:** <1-line note on the real detail used, or "No recent signal found: used playful generic hook">

**Option A: Group Chat / Public Comment (2–3 short lines):**
```text
<short lively post>
```

**Option B: Personal 1:1 DM / WhatsApp (3–5 warm sentences):**
```text
<warmer personal message>
```
```

### Shape Examples (Reference Only: Never Copy Verbatim)
- **Birthday (1:1 DM):** *"Happy birthday, <Name>!! 🎂 Hope the day is a proper break: great food, cake, and zero work pings. Say hi to the family from me. Wishing you an amazing year ahead, and let's catch up over coffee soon 🙂"*
- **Career Milestone / New Role (1:1 DM):** *"Huge congratulations on the new role, <Name>!! 🎉 Well deserved after everything you've built over the last few years. Wishing you all the very best as you settle in, and let's catch up soon!"*

### Banned Phrases (Scan Every Draft)
- `"that's who you are"` / `"it's who you are"`, `"you've earned it"`, `"let yourself be celebrated"`
- `"you make hard problems look calm"`, `"you show up for everyone"`, `"not a small thing"`
- `"onwards and upwards"`, `"keep crushing it"`, `"you've been an inspiration"`
- Multi-paragraph eulogies or virtue lists
- Em dashes anywhere
