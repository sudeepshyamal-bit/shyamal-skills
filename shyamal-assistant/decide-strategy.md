# Decision, Strategy & Lateral Thinking Engine (`decide-strategy.md`)

Loaded for `decide <question>`, `"should I…"`, `"is it worth…"`, `"how should I approach"`, `"plan for"`, `"strategy for"`, or any request to break a deadlock, stress-test a commitment, or generate non-obvious strategic angles.
*(Note: If the request requires building a full multi-source `.html` research dossier across Finances, Holidays, Interviews, or a broad topic, route to `/shyamal-sweep`.)*

---

## Step 1: Graph-First Premise Check & Ponytail Ladder
1. **Read `.state/shyamal-graph/GRAPH_REPORT.md` first** (`<1,200 tokens`) to pull verified God Nodes, `[EXTRACTED]` facts, and active open loops before running any Gmail/Drive/Calendar delta queries. Verify all claims embedded in Sudeep's prompt per `fact-check.md`.
2. **Run the Ponytail 7-Rung Minimal-Intervention Ladder (`references/thinking-models.md` Pillar 1):**
   - Before proposing a complex multi-step strategy, check if **Rung 1 (Skip/YAGNI)**, **Rung 2 (Reuse existing doc/thread)**, **Rung 5 (Delegate to existing advisor/RM)**, or **Rung 6 (One 2-minute call / 2-line message)** solves the entire problem cleanly.
   - Support optional intensity flags: `--lite` (full plan + 1-line simpler alternative), `full` (default), or `--ultra` / `razor` (strip a bloated plan down to the single load-bearing move).

---

## Step 2: Diagnostic Stuck-State Router (Load `references/thinking-models.md` When Triggered)

Diagnose **why** the decision or strategy is non-trivial and apply the matching techniques from `references/thinking-models.md`:

| Problem Shape / Symptom | Diagnosis | Techniques Applied (`references/thinking-models.md`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"Should I do A or B?"* (Both options carry painful trade-offs) | **Binary Trap** | **Concept Fan** (climb to the core goal) + **Synthesis** (engineer **Option C** that captures the upside of both) |
| *"What am I missing?"* / High-stakes financial, career, tax, or property call | **Blind Spot / Fragility** | **Negative Space** (what is unspoken/omitted) + **Worst-Idea Pre-Mortem (Inversion)** + **Ring 2 Unwatched Regulator** |
| Tough negotiation, pushback, interview positioning, or bank/vendor friction | **Counterparty Deadlock** | **Role Swap** (inhabit their exact KPI/compliance pressure) + **Value-Chain / Sequence Flip** + **Ponytail Rung 6 One-Liner** |
| Family vs. work tension, emotional friction, or conflicting priorities | **Multi-Track Collision** | **Six Thinking Hats** (`White / Red / Black / Yellow / Green / Blue`) + **Time Travel (`1yr / 5yr` lens)** |
| UX/Product vision, executive presentation, or creative breakthrough | **Stale Domain Tropes** | **Suppress Rings 0-1** -> **Ring 3 Cross-Domain Raid** + **Combinatorial Fusion / SCAMPER / Provocation (`Po`)** |
| Over-engineered workflow, bloated plan, or too many moving parts | **Complexity Bloat** | **Ponytail `ultra` 7-Rung Razor** + **Constraint Injection** |

**Negative Space & Pre-Mortem Risks (What Could Break):**
- <the unwatched regulator, omitted constraint, or 6-month inversion failure point>

**What this displaces / Cost of a Yes:**
